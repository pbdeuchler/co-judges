Read these numbers with care

  • Few people answer each survey. In {{.Year}}, half of judges got {{medianResponses .Year}} or fewer responses. A handful of people can move a score a lot.
  • Small gaps mean little. Judges a few places apart are about the same.
  • Low does not mean bad. A low rank means lower than other judges. Most judges score well.
  • The commission sees more. It also interviews each judge, watches court and reads rulings. Its recommendation is the fuller review.
